Budget Status Mockups

Same layout in all three: a Sectors card strip on top, then one band per Chapter with Budget, Actual, Encumbrance and Fund Available. Sector cards are live in every mockup — click one or more to filter the bands, and switch the business-unit scope. Figures are the real 2026 Projects Budget Utilization totals as of today (AED, millions).

A

Ring Bands — the sketch, drawn faithfully

Keeps the shapes of the draft: three circles and one square per chapter. The circles are rings, so the Actual and Encumbrance rings visually show their share of Budget, and the square Fund Available tile is the loud green answer at the end of each row.

  • Sector cards: icon, name, budget and a thin utilization bar; selected cards get a check and a brand ring.
  • Best when the reader wants each chapter as its own picture, one at a time.
B

Consumption Bar — where every dirham of the budget sits

Budget is the hero figure on the left of each band; one stacked bar then splits it into Actual, Encumbrance and Fund Available with the three figures under their segments. Two thin rows beneath split the same chapter by business unit (DCT / MSS), so the executive sees both at once without another filter.

  • Sector cards are compact chips, so all of them fit in two rows.
  • Best for comparing chapters against each other and spotting the one that is nearly consumed.
C

Boardroom — dark scorecard for a wall screen

A dark, large-numeral scorecard for projection in a meeting room: an "All chapters" total band on top, then a four-column band per chapter with a consumption rail along its bottom edge. Sector cards sit in a horizontally scrolling strip so the chapters always start above the fold.

  • Same data, same interactions; only the treatment changes. Would ship as a GL app theme option, not a separate page.
  • Best for a single-screen, glance-and-go read by senior management.
