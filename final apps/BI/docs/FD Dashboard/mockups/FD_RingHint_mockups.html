Balance-by-period hint on the chapter rings

Three ways to show a ring's balance by accounting period (YTD, Jan → Sep 2026) as a horizontal bar chart when the reader hovers Budget, Actual, Encumbrance or Fund available. All three use the real Chapter 2 (Opex) totals of the live cube, every business unit. The Actual ring is shown hovered.

1

YTD ladder — one bar per period, on the budget scale

The same dark popover family the Financial Performance KPIs use. Each bar is the YTD balance of that period drawn against that period's YTD budget (the track), so the ladder reads as "how much of the budget was consumed by month-end"; the gold rule is the current period, its figure in gold. Ends with the period-over-period movement.

  • Cheapest to read: length = % of budget, the eye walks down the months.
  • Works identically for the four measures (Fund's ladder shrinks instead of growing).
2

Monthly movement — what each month added

A light card. Each bar is split into the balance brought forward (pale) and the month's movement (strong): the strong part is the month's spend (YTD − prior YTD), so the run-rate is visible at a glance and a flat month (Sep: +0.1M) stands out. Right column: movement, YTD beneath.

  • Best for the "what happened this month" question executives ask first.
  • One scale for the whole ladder (the current YTD = full width).
3

Always-on rail + full ladder on hover

No hover needed to see the shape: a 9-bar rail under each ring's figure shows the period balances at a glance (current period in the measure colour). Hovering the ring or the rail opens the wide ladder with Budget / Balance / % of budget per period and the budget rule per row.

  • Most information, still quiet: the rail is 22 px tall and takes the figure's own colour.
  • Also the only one usable on touch screens, where hover does not exist.
